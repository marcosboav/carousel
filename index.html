06 — Why It Worked

Imperfection Became
The Premium

AI floods every feed now. Visible human flaws read as trust, not defect. Chinese commentators call it 活人感 — "the feel of a living person."

The cultural read

Critics compared its cult trajectory to The Room — calling it "the Chinese Mulholland Drive" of so-bad-it's-good cinema.

Sources: SCMP, Fast Company, Tatler Asia
